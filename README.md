## 💡 Key Takeaways

Throughout ITAI-4375, I gained a deeper understanding of how AI is reshaping healthcare:

1. **Diagnostic vs. Prognostic AI** — I learned the fundamental difference between AI systems that detect existing conditions (like DermaSensor) and those that predict future risk (like Mirai), and why both require rigorous validation before clinical deployment.

2. **Metrics Matter** — Sensitivity, specificity, and AUC are not just numbers; they represent real patients who may be correctly identified or tragically missed.

3. **Health Equity & SDOH** — AI in healthcare cannot succeed without addressing the social determinants that drive health disparities.

4. **NLP's Promise and Pitfalls** — Natural language processing can save clinicians hours of documentation time, but issues around accuracy, bias, and patient privacy demand careful oversight.

5. **Reinforcement Learning for Personalized Medicine** — RL frameworks like automated insulin dosing demonstrate how AI can learn optimal treatment strategies through continuous feedback.
