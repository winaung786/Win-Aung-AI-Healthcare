## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **Diagnostic metrics matter** — High sensitivity catches disease early; specificity prevents unnecessary treatments. Both must be weighed against clinical context.
2. **Prognostic AI unlocks prevention** — Models like Mirai enable years-ahead risk prediction, shifting healthcare from reactive to proactive.
3. **SDOH shape health outcomes** — Addressing food insecurity, housing instability, and education gaps is as important as any clinical intervention.
4. **AI does not replace clinicians** — AI tools augment clinical judgment; human oversight remains essential for safe and ethical care.
5. **NLP drives efficiency** — Automated note summarization and information retrieval reduce clinician burden, but accuracy and privacy safeguards are non-negotiable.
6. **RL offers adaptive treatment** — Reinforcement learning can continuously optimize dosing and therapy, but reward functions must be designed with patient safety at the forefront.
